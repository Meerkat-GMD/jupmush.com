The Night-Eating Wolf and the Paper Village

Unfold paper houses on your board and cross the night against the wolf that eats it.

A deckbuilding roguelite on a grid. Each village you save hands you a small piece of its life, and that piece becomes a paper house card. When monsters block the road, lay your village out on the board and fight beside it.

  • Where you place a house matters. Ovens build heat, pinwheels grow stronger together and a forge strengthens the houses around it.
  • Monsters you can read. Each one strikes by a single rule, and the ground glows red where the next blow lands.
  • A road between battles. Choose your path past engravers, spell libraries, shops, treasure and campfires.
  • Nine battles, one wolf. Eight monsters stand on the road before the night-eating wolf.

Languages: English, Korean
